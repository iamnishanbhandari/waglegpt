# WagleGPT research and editorial notes

Checked 2 November 2026. WagleGPT is independent, unofficial vocabulary satire.

## Attribution standard

Reported vocabulary, checked document vocabulary, and verified spoken quotations are different evidence categories. Generated translations and every humorous example are fictional. The app never marks generated output as a verified quotation and never accepts AI-invented source URLs.
